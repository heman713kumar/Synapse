# Content Rating Answers (IARC Questionnaire)

When you submit to Play Console, you'll fill out the **IARC questionnaire** which assigns ratings for Google Play, ESRB (Americas), PEGI (EU), USK (Germany), and ClassInd (Brazil).

Below are the **recommended answers for Synapse**. Target rating: **Teen / PEGI 12 / IARC 12+** (because of user-generated content + DMs).

## Target Audience & Content

Play Console will ask separately:

- **Target age range:** 13 and up
- **Ads:** None (or "House ads" if you promote Pro inside the app)
- **Mature content:** No
- **Designed for Families program:** No
- **Government / Education target?** No
- **App access:** Some content is gated behind login

---

## Why "Teen" and not "Everyone"

Google requires apps with **user-generated content + private messaging** to be rated at least **Teen** unless you have aggressive moderation tools (e.g. word filters, human review queues, age gates). We do have basic moderation but the safer default for launch is Teen.

If you want to go for **Everyone 10+**:
1. Add stricter word filters
2. Add a moderation queue before content is public
3. Require parental consent flow for users under 13 (COPPA)
4. Re-do the questionnaire

---

## Re-classification

You **must** re-submit the IARC questionnaire if you:
- Add gambling, real-money gaming, or chance-based purchases
- Add live video chat
- Change the content moderation policy materially
- Change the target audience

If your rating changes after an update, you'll get a Play Console warning to update the questionnaire.
